---

## 🎥 Project Overview

Ransomware is a type of malware that:
- Encrypts files
- Locks entire systems
- Demands payment (ransom) to restore access

This video simplifies the concept in **under 60 seconds** for quick learning and awareness.

---

## 📌 What This Video Covers

- ⚠️ What is Ransomware?
- 🧠 How it infects systems (Phishing, malicious links, downloads)
- 💸 Real-world impact (Data loss, financial damage)
- 🛡️ Prevention tips (Backups, awareness, security tools)
